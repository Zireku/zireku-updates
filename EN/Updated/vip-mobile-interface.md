> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Notice:** The checkout button, post-payment connect button, and status badge within the Gateway 1 VIP subscription modal have been refined and scaled down specifically for mobile smartphone displays.

---

### 1. What was the Issue?
On smaller mobile phone screens, the QRIS checkout button and subsequent connection button appeared oversized. Long button labels wrapped awkwardly onto multiple lines, creating clutter. Furthermore, the waiting payment status badge and stepper action button consumed excessive screen height.

---

### 2. What was Fixed?
* Scaled down font sizes and internal padding for checkout and connection buttons to match mobile viewports.
* Enforced single-line text formatting across all action buttons to eliminate unintended line wrapping.
* Compacted the waiting payment status pill and footer stepper navigation buttons into elegant, balanced proportions.
* The update is specifically scoped to Gateway 1 VIP workflows without impacting standard non-VIP nodes.

---

### 3. How does this Affect Users?
* The VIP checkout wizard on smartphone browsers is now clean, proportional, and visually pleasant.
* Buttons and badges fit neatly within screen boundaries without text overflow or excessive height.
* Users can review plans, complete QRIS payments, and proceed to bot pairing effortlessly.

---

### 4. The Result
The VIP bot wizard on Gateway 1 provides a polished, mobile-optimized payment and onboarding experience across all device form factors.
