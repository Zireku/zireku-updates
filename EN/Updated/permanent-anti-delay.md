### 1. What Was the Problem?
Previous anti-delay configurations risked being reset if server components were updated, or missed when users created new rented subbots.

---

### 2. What Was Fixed?
The development team built a permanent 4-layer protection architecture that locks anti-delay settings in place. Whenever the server boots, it automatically verifies and guarantees anti-delay is active for every single bot.

---

### 3. Effect on Users
* Guaranteed that your bot will never experience chat history response delays again.
* Master bots and subbots are automatically fast without requiring any manual setup from users.
* High-speed response times remain permanent even across future platform updates.

---

### 4. Final Result
All bots on the Zireku platform are permanently free from message delays, delivering fast and responsive replies at all times.
